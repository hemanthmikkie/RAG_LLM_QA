# Acme Corporation Global Employee Handbook & Workplace Policy (2026 Edition)

## Section 1: Remote and Hybrid Work Guidelines
Acme Corporation embraces a flexible hybrid work model. Full-time team members are eligible to work remotely up to three (3) days per week, with two (2) designated in-office collaboration days (typically Tuesdays and Thursdays). Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the department VP and People Operations. 

Remote employees are expected to maintain core operational hours from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local time zone. Daily communication must be conducted via official Slack channels and company Google Meet video conferences.

## Section 2: Annual Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leaves
1. Standard PTO Accrual: All full-time regular employees accrue 22 days of paid time off per calendar year, accruing evenly at 1.83 days per month.
2. Carryover Rules: A maximum of five (5) unused PTO days may roll over into the following calendar year. Any additional unused PTO expires on December 31st.
3. Parental Leave: Acme offers sixteen (16) weeks of fully paid parental leave for primary and secondary caregivers following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
4. Wellness Days: Every employee receives three (3) dedicated mental health/wellness days per annum, which do not deduct from their general PTO balance.

## Section 3: Technology and Remote Workspace Stipends
- Home Office Setup Allowance: Each new team member receives a one-time reimbursement of up to $1,200 USD during their first 60 days of employment for home office equipment (ergonomic chair, monitor, desk, peripherals).
- Monthly Connectivity Reimbursement: Remote and hybrid workers are entitled to a monthly internet and mobile stipend of $75 USD, submitted via Expensify by the 25th of each month.
- Hardware Refresh: Standard engineering laptops (MacBook Pro 16-inch M-series or ThinkPad P1) are refreshed every three (3) years.

## Section 4: Travel and Expense Reimbursement
1. Meals Per Diem: When traveling for approved client or team on-site business, the daily meal allowance is capped at $85 USD per day. Alcohol expenses require prior managerial authorization.
2. Air Travel: Domestic flights under 5 hours must be booked in Economy or Economy Plus. International flights exceeding 7 continuous hours are eligible for Business Class booking.
3. Receipt Threshold: Detailed itemized receipts are mandatory for all individual expenses exceeding $25 USD.

## Section 5: Code of Conduct & Information Security
- Confidentiality: Company proprietary code, customer financial records, and internal strategy decks are strictly confidential. Storing company source code or customer data on unencrypted personal drives or uploading to unauthorized third-party public AI services is strictly prohibited.
- Passwords & 2FA: Two-factor authentication (2FA) using hardware security keys or authenticator apps (YubiKey or Google Authenticator) is required across all corporate logins. SMS-based 2FA is deprecated.
